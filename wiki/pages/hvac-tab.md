# HVAC tab

The HVAC tab shares the service equipment model and generic router with Plumbing while keeping its own tools, panel
and canvas mode. It derives heating, radiant, refrigerant and duct runs and exposes their quantities in the Materials
takeoff. Equipment is also rendered in elevations and 3-D.

Load estimates are rule-of-thumb planning values. The app does not choose between boilers, heat pumps, splits,
radiators or radiant systems, and it does not guess equipment placement. Power requirements are declared links to the
[Electricity tab](electricity-tab.md), where the user creates the actual rated point and circuit.
