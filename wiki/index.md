# architect wiki — index

The catalog of this wiki: every page, grouped by type, one line each (`- [Title](path) — summary`). Conventions:
[SCHEMA.md](SCHEMA.md). History: [log.md](log.md).

## Overview

- [Overview](overview.md) — architect at a glance: a local browser app for sketching a house (walls at their thickness and height, open edges with optional Roman or metal colonnades, ground-level outlines, named areas (rooms, or lots of land with the share covered) with floor and covered totals, doors, windows, gates and fireplaces with chimneys, and furniture: counters, sinks, bathtubs, showers, toilets, bidets, beds, sofas, tables, chairs; wall-side and floor finishes from a materials catalogue) over a calibrated photo of a land survey, with the sun and wall shadows by season and time, in tabs over one plan (the Floor plan, a Roof plan of drawn flat, shed, gable and hip roofs, four auto-drawn Elevations and a basic 3-D Exterior); architecture, stack, build/run/test, where to start and open items.

## Components

- [Plan model and file format](pages/plan-model.md) — The saved plan: a zod schema in src/model/plan.ts (format "architect/plan", schemaVersion 1, meters with +y down, the site's north and location for the sun, per-layer vertex/segment graphs with segment kinds, optional wall thickness and height, wall-side finishes and open-edge colonnades, named areas (rooms, or lots of land) with optional floor finishes, styled doors/windows/gates/fireplaces hosted by segments, drawn roofs and free-standing furniture with kitchen sinks kept in counters, embedded base image), with parsing, migrations and serialization in planFile.ts.
- [Editor store and state](pages/editor-store.md) — src/state/: the EditorStore (immutable plan, undo via edit/preview/endGesture, editor-only state such as the open tab, active layer, tool, line kind, door/window/gate/fireplace style to place, column style, furniture and material picks, roof type to draw, view and prompt), user-level actions (openTab, roofTheHouse, applyColonnade, placeFurnitureAt, applyFinish among them), derived data cached per object (derived.ts: regions, wall thicknesses and shapes, covered area, 3-D solids), files (File System Access API, IndexedDB draft, image import) and per-browser prefs (per-style opening widths, folded panel sections, sun view settings, the View section's toggles, the Exterior's roof toggle).
- [Canvas and tools](pages/canvas-and-tools.md) — The SVG canvas of the Floor and Roof tabs in src/ui/canvas/ (mode floor or roof): Canvas.tsx routes pointer and key events to plain tool controllers in tools/ that return OverlayData and takes library drops (openings, colonnades, furniture and materials); Scene.tsx draws the plan (walls filled at their thickness unless Thick walls is off, area fills in their floor finish (checkered ones as SVG patterns) unless View > Materials is off, area labels with the lines the View section shows), Columns.tsx the colonnades, Finishes.tsx the wall finishes, Furniture.tsx the furniture symbols, Sun.tsx the sun dial and wall shadows, Roofs.tsx the roof plan, and Overlay.tsx the tool feedback, at constant screen size; the select tool's hit order.
- [Walls: thickness, height and joins](pages/walls.md) — src/model/walls.ts: lines stay centrelines; a wall's optional thickness (absent = automatic: 0.30 m facing outside, 0.15 m between rooms) and height (absent = 2.70 m) live on the segment; layerWalls draws walls filled at their thickness with mitred or bevelled joins, cut by openings, with per-wall footprints; Thick walls toggle, face hit tests, updateWalls and the Selection panel's wall fields.
- [Doors, windows and gates](pages/doors-and-windows.md) — Openings hosted by a segment (src/model/openings.ts): layout, wall gaps (fireplaces cut none), placement targets with middle and 5 cm snapping, edits that keep them on their walls, the Door/Window/Gate/Fireplace tools, the library's Doors, Windows, Gates and Fireplaces groups, dragging, drawing and the Selection panel. Styles and fireplaces have their own pages.
- [Door, window and gate styles](pages/opening-styles.md) — Door styles (single, double, sliding), window styles (fixed, sliding, double-hung, awning, casement), gate styles (single, double, sliding) and the masonry fireplace: the style field and its defaults, the STYLES table with usual widths, hasSide/hasHinge/slides/cutsWall, sliding-gate direction and parkingRoom, and openingParts, the one source of symbol geometry for both the plan and the library icons (the fireplace's icon excepted).
- [Fireplaces](pages/fireplaces.md) — A fireplace is a fourth opening kind (FireplaceSchema, style masonry, 1.20 m) hosted by a wall on its swing side that cuts no gap (cutsWall false); fireplaceShape/besideWall (box, firebox, hearth, flue) feed the plan symbol, the roof plan's chimney and the solids; wall-only targets; picked anywhere on its surround; brick and stone in 3-D with a chimney 1 m over the roof at its flue.
- [Colonnades](pages/colonnades.md) — Columns along an open edge (SegmentSchema.colonnade: roman or metal, a maximum spacing): src/model/colonnades.ts (COLUMNS sizes, columnSpots shared out evenly, layerColonnades leaving out ends a wall holds, heldByWall), setColonnade, the Colonnade tool and the library's Colonnades box, ColonnadeLayer on the plan, ColonnadeFields in the Selection panel, and stone or steel beams and columns in 3-D.
- [Furniture and fixtures](pages/furniture.md) — Free-standing pieces in layer.furniture (ids f<n>): counters and kitchen islands (a counter style), sinks (outdoor, kitchen, bathroom), bathtubs (alcove, clawfoot, drop in, corner) and showers with a movable shower head, toilets, bidets, beds (single to king), sofas (armchair to an L-shaped sectional that flips), square, round and coffee tables, chairs; src/model/furniture.ts (FURNITURE catalogue, placements, furnitureTarget snapping to wall faces, counters, tables and sofas, the kitchen sink laid out from its counter, shower rim positions incl. a corner tub's curve, sofa outlines, plan symbols), furniture3d.ts (blocks in 3-D), the Furniture tool, select-tool drags, R to turn, the library boxes and FurnitureDetails.
- [Materials and finishes](pages/materials-and-finishes.md) — A materials catalogue (src/model/materials.ts: 29 MATERIALS by family, each with the surfaces it suits and whether it works indoors and/or outdoors, a checkered tile drawn as squares; suits, materialGroups, findMaterial) and finishes on wall sides and area floors (src/model/finishes.ts: sides named left/right looking a→b, a side outdoor unless the region past its face is an indoor room, lots counting as outdoor; finishProblem; segment.finish and area.floor in the plan), set by the Material tool, library drags or the Selection panel, drawn in plan (Finishes.tsx, AreaFills; off with View > Materials), in 3-D and in the elevations.
- [Sun and shadows](pages/sun.md) — The sun over the plan: pure solar math in src/model/sun.ts (position, sunrise/sunset, plan angle, shadow offset, hemisphere-aware seasons), the plan's saved site (north, latitude, longitude, UTC offset), the North tool, SunDial and SunShadows in src/ui/canvas/Sun.tsx and the Sun panel; season, time and clouds are per-browser prefs, shared with the Exterior tab's 3-D sun.
- [House solids: the plan in 3-D](pages/house-solids.md) — src/model/solids.ts: houseSolids(plan) turns the visible layers into solids made of flat faces with explicit outward normals (kind, faces, bottom, top, palette material, ref; round facets marked smooth; face types and builders in faces.ts): walls at their own height, lintels, sills, frames, glass and leaves of openings, floors of named areas, the roofs drawn on the Roof tab with parapets, exterior walls filled up to a roof's underside (gable ends), beams under roofs along open edges on columns, colonnades (Roman or metal columns under a beam), masonry fireplaces with chimneys through the roof, and furniture (furniture3d.ts); wall faces tagged per side with their finishes (reveals split at the centreline) and floors with theirs; cached per plan by getSolids.
- [Roofs: model and geometry](pages/roofs.md) — Drawn roofs in layer.roofs (RoofSchema: outline, type flat/shed/gable/hip, pitch, height at the eaves sides, overhang, reference edge, parapet; ids r<n>) and src/model/roofs.ts: each slope a plane f(p) = distance in from an eaves line, underside = height + tan(pitch)·min f, faces by clipping the eaves polygon with half-planes, the underside along a wall, new-roof defaults, the hip convexity warning and unionOutlines for Roof the house.
- [Roof tab (roof plan)](pages/roof-tab.md) — The Roof tab: src/ui/RoofTab.tsx (Select V / Roof R / Pan H, the New roofs type = EditorState.roofType, Roof the house, Snap) over the shared Canvas in roof mode (floor plan faded, Roofs.tsx draws slopes shaded by aspect, eaves, dashed outline under an overhang, downhill arrows, labels, fireplaces' chimneys), the roof and roofSelect tools, RoofPanel (Box roofs), and the actions openTab, addRoofOutline and roofTheHouse.
- [Elevations tab](pages/elevations.md) — Four facades drawn automatically from the house solids, view only: src/model/elevations.ts (main direction from the walls, four views square to it named by compass bearing through site.north, back-face culling on explicit normals, painter's sort by each face's nearest point, facing and smooth per polygon, level marks) and src/ui/ElevationsTab.tsx (fills shaded by facing, in a finish's colour where there is one, smooth facets without outlines, All four or one, layer visibility checkboxes, Save SVG as a standalone drawing).
- [Exterior tab (3-D view)](pages/exterior-tab.md) — The Exterior tab: src/ui/exterior/ExteriorTab.tsx (camera presets, Roof toggle for the drawn roofs and parapets, sun season and time, Save image) over a three.js view in src/render/ (house.ts triangulates each solid face in its own plane and winds it to the face's explicit normal, plan y as three z; engine.ts, adapted from the Forge H1 planner, lights it with the plan's sun and real shadows, draws on demand and keeps the camera pose across tab switches); three.js is a separate chunk loaded only when the tab opens.

## Concepts

- [Base image placement and calibration](pages/base-image-calibration.md) — How the survey photo sits in the world (centre + R·scale·(uv − size/2)), leveling by two points, the least-squares scale fit over all known lengths with a per-length error %, crop, and editImageInPlace keeping the image still on screen.
- [Snapping and connections](pages/snapping-and-connections.md) — findSnap's priorities (vertex > midpoint > edge), the Shift 45° constraint with align and ray/segment intersection, and how snaps become topology only within a layer (vertexForSnap splits segments; dropping a dragged vertex connects it).
- [Regions](pages/regions.md) — src/model/regions.ts: the outlines a layer's lines enclose, as faces of a planar arrangement (split at crossings and touches, a wall wins over other kinds, edges keep their segment ids) traced with half-edges; layerFaces adds which regions are indoor, which walls face outside and each wall's thickness; a Region has holes, area to centrelines, floor inside the wall faces, wallIds, boundary lengths by kind, regionAt and a lazy cached label point.
- [Areas](pages/areas.md) — An area is a named point ({name, at} in layer.areas) whose outline is the region of its layer's lines enclosing the point, Revit-room style; a room shows its floor area inside the wall faces and a lot (lot: true) its area to the lot lines (areaSize); coveredArea totals indoor rooms with their walls, lots excluded; regions and placed areas are cached per object in src/state/derived.ts (planAreas per plan); one area per region, "not enclosed" when the lines open; Area tool and label dragging.
- [Lots](pages/lots.md) — An area marked as a lot (AreaSchema.lot: true, set by setAreaLot or the Selection panel's Lot checkbox) is land, not a room: it counts as outdoor even when its boundary walls close it in, is sized to its boundary centrelines (areaSize), is left out of the floor and covered totals, is summed in the Areas panel's Lots and Lot covered % (FOS) rows, gets no 3-D slab unless finished (then a 1 cm ground cover), and has its label placed clear of the other layers' areas (planAreas).

## Decisions

- [Stack adapted from the Forge H1 planner](pages/forge-h1-stack.md) — Architect uses the Forge H1 planner's stack (React, TypeScript, Vite, zod, Vitest, oxlint, and three.js for 3-D) and adapts its store, prefs, files, fields, NoticeBar, CSS tokens, SVG canvas and render engine, instead of the no-build vanilla JS app it started as.
- [View fitted once and stored](pages/view-fitted-once.md) — The canvas fits the plan once and stores that view, instead of following planBounds on every render, which made cropping run away and shifted the view while drawing.
- [Open edges and outlines are segment kinds, not layers](pages/segment-kind-not-layer.md) — A segment is a wall, an open edge (porch front, colonnade) or an outline (ground-level edge with no height: pool, paving, deck, planter): one attribute (kind: 'open' | 'outline', absent = wall), not separate layers, because topology and regions exist only within a layer; drawn by drafting convention (walls heavy, open edges dashed, outlines thin), all three bound areas, only walls cast shadows.
- [Doors and windows are hosted by a segment](pages/openings-hosted-by-segment.md) — A door or window is an opening in layer.openings hosted by one segment at a centre offset from its a end, not a segment kind between two vertices on the wall line (which would fragment the wall and not follow a moved corner); when the wall's length changes it keeps its distance to the nearer end.
- [Indoor means unreachable from outside](pages/indoor-reachability-rule.md) — A region is indoor unless the outside reaches it by crossing open edges or outlines (a walk over regions from the unbounded face); a wall with a non-indoor region or the outside on either side is exterior (automatic 0.30 m). Chosen over "walled on every side", which turned an open-plan kitchen and living room into outdoors.
- [Tabs over one plan](pages/tabs-over-one-plan.md) — A two-row bar: row 1 has the tabs (Foundation, Floor, Roof, Elevations, Exterior, Interior, Plumbing, Electricity, Gas, Materials; Floor, Roof, Elevations and Exterior built, the rest disabled), row 2 the open tab's actions; every tab works on the same plan with one undo history, the open tab is editor state (not saved), and openTab switches tools and selection between the floor and roof plans.
- [Exterior kept basic; roof and elevations first](pages/exterior-kept-basic.md) — At the user's direction the Exterior tab stays basic (fixed palette, a roof on/off toggle, no materials UI or materials fields in the plan); a drafted materials catalogue with a surfaces × indoor/outdoor compatibility table and exterior/facade/floor schema fields was removed; the Roof tab and Elevations came next and were built the same day, replacing the automatic flat roof with drawn roofs; superseded in part on 2026-11-04 by wall and floor finishes from a materials catalogue, the palette staying as the base.
- [Roofs are drawn outlines; slopes are planes](pages/roofs-drawn-outlines.md) — Roofs are outlines drawn on a roof plan (flat, shed, gable, hip), not one roof per area, so a roof can span rooms and overhang walls; each slope is a plane given as the distance in from an eaves line and the underside is the lowest of them; the automatic flat roof was removed; a straight skeleton for hips on any outline was rejected for now.
- [Colonnades are an open-edge setting; fireplaces are an opening kind](pages/colonnade-setting-fireplace-opening.md) — A colonnade is an optional attribute of an open-edge segment (colonnade: {style, spacing}) and a fireplace a fourth opening kind (after door, window and gate) that cuts no gap, so both reuse what segments and openings already do (splits, moves, following walls, selection, library); a new hosted collection with its own ids was rejected for each, because it would need the split/prune/follow/move upkeep openings have.
- [Furniture stands free; a kitchen sink is hosted by its counter](pages/furniture-free-standing.md) — Furniture is a per-layer collection of free-standing pieces (at, angle, width, depth) that snap to a wall face, a counter or a table only when put down or dragged, not records hosted by a wall segment like openings (right for doors, wrong for beds and tables that stand anywhere); only the kitchen sink is hosted, by its counter (counter id + offset, no position of its own), rather than an absolute position plus a counter id, which would be two sources of truth; a kitchen island is a counter style, not a new kind, so sinks go in it unchanged.

## Conventions

- [Code conventions](pages/code-conventions.md) — Rules inherited from the Forge H1 planner: pure model layer with no import cycles, state never imports ui, three.js only in src/render, explicit face normals in 3-D, immutable edits through layer drafts (same plan = no undo step), derived data cached by object identity, saved data in the plan, UI state in the store and view settings in prefs, foldable panel sections with Box ids, erasable-only TypeScript, tools without React refs, compound .opening.<kind> CSS selectors, oxlint rules (helpers out of component files), ids and rounding.

## Gotchas

- [Playwright flow-check pitfalls](pages/playwright-flow-checks.md) — Python Playwright against the SVG canvas: SVG elements have no innerText, python -I ignores PYTHONIOENCODING, clicks land on whole CSS pixels (assert stored values), library icons share the .opening class, library button names include their counts, an opening's swing side depends on the wall's drawing direction, a passing click may only have missed an overlay, the start plan's autosave overwrites an injected draft, the user's draft may show or hide its photo (shown, F frames the whole survey and area labels take clicks) and keeps changing, folded sections render nothing, panel sections must be found by data-box not position or text (Library boxes nest), a role name "Wall" also matches the "Walls" toggle, WebGL needs SwiftShader flags and reads back blank (measure screenshots), keyboard shortcuts are ignored while a checkbox has focus, React leaves an empty style="" (check e.style.fill), a long-running dev server serves stale HMR module copies, a stopped background vite can keep its port, layer names are inputs, tool buttons' text has a newline before the key, flows on the user's live plan need a pinned fixture, and bash heredocs mangle escapes in inline Python.
- [Bash hook blocks `*.key` patterns and path colons](pages/bash-hook-key-paths.md) — A PreToolUse hook in the agent environment blocks Bash commands that mention *.key paths (which also matches JavaScript like event.key in inline scripts) and commands with a path followed by a colon (read as a Windows alternate data stream); put such text in a script file and run the file.
- [Notices float over the canvas](pages/notices-float-over-canvas.md) — NoticeBar used to sit in the page layout above the canvas, so an info notice auto-dismissing after 7 s moved the canvas ~40 px under the cursor and clicks mid-drawing landed elsewhere; notices now float over it (position absolute inside .app-main).
- [Welcome card swallowed canvas clicks](pages/welcome-card-swallowed-clicks.md) — On an empty plan the centred welcome card (.empty-state, "Start from a photo…") caught canvas clicks, so a first line drawn through the middle lost its second point; the card now has pointer-events: none except its button and shows only while Select or Pan is active.
