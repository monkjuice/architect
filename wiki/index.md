## Overview

- [Overview](overview.md) — architect at a glance: a local browser app for sketching a house (walls at their thickness and height, open edges, ground-level outlines, named areas with floor and covered totals, doors, windows and gates) over a calibrated photo of a land survey, with the sun and wall shadows by season and time, in tabs over one plan (the Floor plan and a basic 3-D Exterior); architecture, stack, build/run/test, where to start and open items.

## Components

- [Plan model and file format](pages/plan-model.md) — The saved plan: a zod schema in src/model/plan.ts (format "architect/plan", schemaVersion 1, meters with +y down, the site's north and location for the sun, per-layer vertex/segment graphs with segment kinds and optional wall thickness and height, named areas and styled doors/windows/gates hosted by segments, embedded base image), with parsing, migrations and serialization in planFile.ts.
- [Editor store and state](pages/editor-store.md) — src/state/: the EditorStore (immutable plan, undo via edit/preview/endGesture, editor-only state such as the open tab, active layer, tool, line kind, door/window/gate style to place, view and prompt), user-level actions, derived data cached per object (derived.ts: regions, wall thicknesses and shapes, covered area, 3-D solids), files (File System Access API, IndexedDB draft, image import) and per-browser prefs (per-style opening widths, folded panel sections, sun view settings, the View section's toggles, the Exterior's roof toggle).
- [Canvas and tools](pages/canvas-and-tools.md) — The Floor tab's SVG canvas in src/ui/canvas/: Canvas.tsx routes pointer and key events to plain tool controllers in tools/ that return OverlayData; Scene.tsx draws the plan (walls filled at their thickness unless Thick walls is off, area labels with the lines the View section shows), Sun.tsx the sun dial and wall shadows, and Overlay.tsx the tool feedback, at constant screen size.
- [Walls: thickness, height and joins](pages/walls.md) — src/model/walls.ts: lines stay centrelines; a wall's optional thickness (absent = automatic: 0.30 m facing outside, 0.15 m between rooms) and height (absent = 2.70 m) live on the segment; layerWalls draws walls filled at their thickness with mitred or bevelled joins, cut by openings, with per-wall footprints; Thick walls toggle, face hit tests, updateWalls and the Selection panel's wall fields.
- [Doors, windows and gates](pages/doors-and-windows.md) — Openings hosted by a segment (src/model/openings.ts): layout, wall gaps, placement targets with middle and 5 cm snapping, edits that keep them on their walls, the Door/Window/Gate tools, the library's Doors, Windows and Gates groups, dragging, drawing and the Selection panel. Styles have their own page.
- [Door, window and gate styles](pages/opening-styles.md) — Door styles (single, double, sliding), window styles (fixed, sliding, double-hung, awning, casement) and gate styles (single, double, sliding): the style field and its defaults, the STYLES table with usual widths, hasSide/hasHinge/slides, sliding-gate direction and parkingRoom, and openingParts, the one source of symbol geometry for both the plan and the library icons.
- [Sun and shadows](pages/sun.md) — The sun over the plan: pure solar math in src/model/sun.ts (position, sunrise/sunset, plan angle, shadow offset, hemisphere-aware seasons), the plan's saved site (north, latitude, longitude, UTC offset), the North tool, SunDial and SunShadows in src/ui/canvas/Sun.tsx and the Sun panel; season, time and clouds are per-browser prefs, shared with the Exterior tab's 3-D sun.
- [House solids: the plan in 3-D](pages/house-solids.md) — src/model/solids.ts: houseSolids(plan) turns the visible layers into prisms (outline, holes, bottom, top, palette material, ref): walls at their thickness rising to the roof they carry, lintels, sills, frames, glass and leaves of openings, floors of named areas, flat roofs over rooms and galleries, beams and columns along open edges; cached per plan by getSolids.
- [Exterior tab (3-D view)](pages/exterior-tab.md) — The Exterior tab: src/ui/exterior/ExteriorTab.tsx (camera presets, Roof toggle, sun season and time, Save image) over a three.js view in src/render/ (house.ts extrudes the solids with plan y as three z; engine.ts, adapted from the Forge H1 planner, lights it with the plan's sun and real shadows, draws on demand and keeps the camera pose across tab switches); three.js is a separate chunk loaded only when the tab opens.

## Concepts

- [Base image placement and calibration](pages/base-image-calibration.md) — How the survey photo sits in the world (centre + R·scale·(uv − size/2)), leveling by two points, the least-squares scale fit over all known lengths with a per-length error %, crop, and editImageInPlace keeping the image still on screen.
- [Snapping and connections](pages/snapping-and-connections.md) — findSnap's priorities (vertex > midpoint > edge), the Shift 45° constraint with align and ray/segment intersection, and how snaps become topology only within a layer (vertexForSnap splits segments; dropping a dragged vertex connects it).
- [Regions](pages/regions.md) — src/model/regions.ts: the outlines a layer's lines enclose, as faces of a planar arrangement (split at crossings and touches, a wall wins over other kinds, edges keep their segment ids) traced with half-edges; layerFaces adds which regions are indoor, which walls face outside and each wall's thickness; a Region has holes, area to centrelines, floor inside the wall faces, wallIds, boundary lengths by kind, regionAt and a lazy cached label point.
- [Areas](pages/areas.md) — An area is a named point ({name, at} in layer.areas) whose outline is the region of its layer's lines enclosing the point, Revit-room style; sizes shown are floor areas inside the wall faces, and coveredArea totals indoor rooms with their walls; regions and placed areas are cached per object in src/state/derived.ts; one area per region, "not enclosed" when the lines open; Area tool and label dragging.

## Decisions

- [Stack adapted from the Forge H1 planner](pages/forge-h1-stack.md) — Architect uses the Forge H1 planner's stack (React, TypeScript, Vite, zod, Vitest, oxlint, and three.js for 3-D) and adapts its store, prefs, files, fields, NoticeBar, CSS tokens, SVG canvas and render engine, instead of the no-build vanilla JS app it started as.
- [View fitted once and stored](pages/view-fitted-once.md) — The canvas fits the plan once and stores that view, instead of following planBounds on every render, which made cropping run away and shifted the view while drawing.
- [Open edges and outlines are segment kinds, not layers](pages/segment-kind-not-layer.md) — A segment is a wall, an open edge (porch front, colonnade) or an outline (ground-level edge with no height: pool, paving, deck, planter): one attribute (kind: 'open' | 'outline', absent = wall), not separate layers, because topology and regions exist only within a layer; drawn by drafting convention (walls heavy, open edges dashed, outlines thin), all three bound areas, only walls cast shadows.
- [Doors and windows are hosted by a segment](pages/openings-hosted-by-segment.md) — A door or window is an opening in layer.openings hosted by one segment at a centre offset from its a end, not a segment kind between two vertices on the wall line (which would fragment the wall and not follow a moved corner); when the wall's length changes it keeps its distance to the nearer end.
- [Indoor means unreachable from outside](pages/indoor-reachability-rule.md) — A region is indoor unless the outside reaches it by crossing open edges or outlines (a walk over regions from the unbounded face); a wall with a non-indoor region or the outside on either side is exterior (automatic 0.30 m). Chosen over "walled on every side", which turned an open-plan kitchen and living room into outdoors.
- [Tabs over one plan](pages/tabs-over-one-plan.md) — A two-row bar: row 1 has the tabs (Foundation, Floor, Roof, Elevations, Exterior, Interior, Plumbing, Electricity, Gas, Materials; only Floor and Exterior built, the rest disabled), row 2 the open tab's actions; every tab works on the same plan with one undo history, and the open tab is editor state, not saved.
- [Exterior kept basic; roof and elevations first](pages/exterior-kept-basic.md) — At the user's direction the Exterior tab stays basic (fixed palette, automatic flat roof with an on/off toggle, no materials UI, plan format unchanged); a drafted materials catalogue with a surfaces × indoor/outdoor compatibility table and exterior/facade/floor schema fields was removed; the Roof tab and Elevations come next.

## Conventions

- [Code conventions](pages/code-conventions.md) — Rules inherited from the Forge H1 planner: pure model layer, state never imports ui, three.js only in src/render, immutable edits through layer drafts (same plan = no undo step), derived data cached by object identity, saved data in the plan, UI state in the store and view settings in prefs, foldable panel sections with Box ids, erasable-only TypeScript, tools without React refs, compound .opening.<kind> CSS selectors, oxlint rules, ids and rounding.

## Gotchas

- [Playwright flow-check pitfalls](pages/playwright-flow-checks.md) — Python Playwright against the SVG canvas: <text> has no innerText, python -I ignores PYTHONIOENCODING, clicks land on whole CSS pixels (assert stored values), library icons share the .opening class, library button names include their counts, an opening's swing side depends on the wall's drawing direction, a passing click may only have missed an overlay, the start plan's autosave overwrites an injected draft, the user's draft hides its photo and keeps changing, folded sections render nothing, panel sections must be found by data-box not position, WebGL needs SwiftShader flags and reads back blank (measure screenshots), and Ctrl+Z is ignored while a checkbox has focus.
- [Bash hook blocks `*.key` patterns and path colons](pages/bash-hook-key-paths.md) — A PreToolUse hook in the agent environment blocks Bash commands that mention *.key paths (which also matches JavaScript like event.key in inline scripts) and commands with a path followed by a colon (read as a Windows alternate data stream); put such text in a script file and run the file.
- [Notices float over the canvas](pages/notices-float-over-canvas.md) — NoticeBar used to sit in the page layout above the canvas, so an info notice auto-dismissing after 7 s moved the canvas ~40 px under the cursor and clicks mid-drawing landed elsewhere; notices now float over it (position absolute inside .app-main).
- [Welcome card swallowed canvas clicks](pages/welcome-card-swallowed-clicks.md) — On an empty plan the centred welcome card (.empty-state, "Start from a photo…") caught canvas clicks, so a first line drawn through the middle lost its second point; the card now has pointer-events: none except its button and shows only while Select or Pan is active.
